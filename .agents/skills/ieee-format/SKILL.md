# IEEE Standard Format for Research Papers & Manuscripts

## Overview
The IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) conference format is the global standard for engineering, computer science, and applied technology research papers. It is characterized by a high-density, rigorous 2-column layout on A4 / US Letter paper.

---

## Document Layout & Typography Architecture

### 1. Page Geometry & Grid
- **Paper Size**: A4 (210mm × 297mm) or US Letter (8.5" × 11").
- **Margins**:
  - Top: `0.75"` (19mm)
  - Bottom: `1.0"` (25.4mm)
  - Left / Right: `0.625"` (15.9mm)
- **Column Setup**:
  - Top Block (Title, Authors, Abstract, Keywords): **1 Single Column (Full Width)**
  - Body Content (Introduction through References): **2 Equal-Width Columns**
  - Column Gap: `0.2"` to `0.25"` (5mm–6.3mm / 720 twips)

### 2. Typographic Hierarchy (Times New Roman)
| Component | Font Size | Styling | Alignment | Spacing / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper Title** | 24 pt | Regular / Bold | Centered | Line spacing: 1.0, No subtitles in Xplore |
| **Author Names** | 10–11 pt | Regular / Bold | Centered | 1 to 6 authors in 3-column / 2-row table |
| **Affiliations & Email** | 9–10 pt | Italic (dept/org), Regular (City/Email) | Centered | Clean minimal table, no outer borders |
| **Abstract Heading** | 9–10 pt | **Bold Italic** (`Abstract—`) | Justified | Run-in with em-dash (`—`) |
| **Abstract Text** | 9–10 pt | Bold / Regular | Justified | 150–250 words summary |
| **Keywords Heading** | 9–10 pt | **Bold Italic** (`Keywords—`) | Justified | Run-in with em-dash (`—`) |
| **Keywords List** | 9–10 pt | Italic / Regular | Justified | 4–6 comma-separated domain keywords |
| **Heading 1 (Primary)** | 10 pt | **Bold, Small Caps / Uppercase** | Centered | Roman numerals (`I. INTRODUCTION`) |
| **Heading 2 (Secondary)** | 10 pt | *Italic* | Left-aligned | Capital letters (`A. System Architecture`) |
| **Heading 3 (Tertiary)** | 10 pt | *Italic* | Left-aligned | Numbered (`1) Mathematical Formulation:`) |
| **Body Paragraphs** | 10 pt | Regular | Justified | 1.05 line spacing, 0.2" first-line indent |
| **Equations** | 10 pt | Italic symbols | Centered | Numbered flush right `(1)`, `(2)` |
| **Table Titles** | 8–9 pt | Small Caps / Uppercase | Centered | Placed **ABOVE** table: `TABLE I. ...` |
| **Figure Captions** | 8–9 pt | Regular | Centered / Left | Placed **BELOW** figure: `Fig. 1. ...` |
| **References** | 8.5–9 pt | Regular | Justified | Centered `REFERENCES`, numbered `[1]`, `[2]` |

## IEEE Section Taxonomy
Standard research papers must follow the formal empirical structure:
1. **Abstract & Keywords**
2. **I. INTRODUCTION** (Context, Problem Statement, Scope, Contributions)
3. **II. RELATED WORK & FOUNDATIONAL LITERATURE** (Taxonomy of prior methods, Gaps)
4. **III. PROPOSED METHODOLOGY & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE** (Formulation, Algorithms, System Model)
5. **IV. EXPERIMENTAL SETUP & EMPIRICAL RESULTS** (Datasets, Baselines, Benchmarks, Tables)
6. **V. DISCUSSION, ABLATION ANALYSIS & THREATS TO VALIDITY** (Critical evaluation, Trade-offs)
7. **VI. CONCLUSION & FUTURE WORK** (Synthesis, Impact, Open Directions)
8. **ACKNOWLEDGMENT** (Optional sponsor or grant acknowledgments)
9. **REFERENCES** (Consecutively numbered citations in brackets `[1]`, `[2]`)

## Reference Citation Rules
- In-text citation: `[1]`, `[2]`, `[1]–[3]`. Sentence punctuation follows the bracket: `as demonstrated in [2].`
- Never write `Ref. [3]` or `reference [3]` unless at the very start of a sentence: `Reference [3] was the first to...`
- Reference format:
  - Journal: `[1] G. Eason, B. Noble, and I. N. Sneddon, "On certain integrals of Lipschitz-Hankel type involving products of Bessel functions," Phil. Trans. Roy. Soc. London, vol. A247, pp. 529–551, April 1955.`
  - Conference: `[2] D. P. Kingma and M. Welling, "Auto-encoding variational Bayes," in Proc. Int. Conf. Learn. Represent. (ICLR), 2014, pp. 1–14.`
  - Book: `[3] J. Clerk Maxwell, A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism, 3rd ed., vol. 2. Oxford: Clarendon, 1892, pp. 68–73.`
